REG. 1962 • ACT I

Cuihu High School — West Hallway

The wet cedar beneath your heels does not yield; it groans with the weight of someone who shouldn't be here. Ahead, the solitary hanging bulb spits a brief orange tremor, humming against the damp ceiling plaster.

A draft brings the scent of burnt incense and drenched paper notices. From behind the frosted glass of the Principal’s office, fifty yards into the soot, something scrapes softly across the floor. Once. Then again.
